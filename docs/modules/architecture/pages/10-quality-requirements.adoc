= 10. Quality Requirements

The matrix below is the closed M1 quality-scenario set. Numerical limits and
detailed contracts are linked to their normative requirements rather than
copied. No elapsed-time target, tool-specific test prescription, or screenshot
equality is introduced.

[cols="2,5,4,6,6,6",options="header"]

submission.

later attempt may select the newer tip.
|Controlled resolution evidence proves ADR 0005's second request omits every

|3. Anonymous operation
|The user submits invalid input or an accepted URL whose repository is missing,
private, access denied, or otherwise anonymously unavailable.

service credential. Unavailable cases remain indistinguishable and never offer
authentication recovery.
|Boundary evidence proves no interaction for invalid input and no authorization

|6. Cancellation
|The user cancels `Loading`, including when cancellation and a result are tied.
|Current attempt may or may not already have a selected identity; the input
draft may have been edited.
|The exact `Cancelled` state is observable: draft preserved, identity and city
absent, and every late outcome rejected. No elapsed-time target is defined.
ADRs 0005 and 0006 fix their resolution/retrieval response and
application-owned cleanup mechanisms; cancellation remains a lifecycle result,
not a timing failure.
|A controlled race or tie scenario proves cancellation wins observable
publication and late results change nothing. Instrumented evidence cancels
during each REST stream, raw streaming and cutoff, post-JSON, post-digest,
decode, normalization, and pending cleanup; it observes attempt-signal abort,
feasible body cancellation, reader-lock release in `finally`, required
eligibility checks, complete application-owned cleanup, and suppression of
every later request or stage without reclassifying the abort.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/441[#441]
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#states[M1-STATE-4 and
M1-STATE-5] and the downstream atomicity invariants; focused retrieval evidence
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/454[#454] and integrated
evidence https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|7. Replacement and atomic publication
|A user submits again while any prior state or attempt exists, and the
replacement succeeds or fails at any stage.
|Current session with a prior loading, success, failure, or cancelled state.
|The new submission invalidates prior work before validation; only the current
attempt can publish. Failure shows no stale or partial city; success publishes
the complete city and identity together.
|End-to-end replacement, late-result, stage-failure, and
successful-publication evidence proves one current outcome and no partial or
stale output. Controlled retrieval evidence also proves pending submissions
coalesce to the latest and no replacement provider request or workspace starts
until old reads, cancellation, non-abortable work, locks, and application-owned
cleanup settle.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/441[#441]
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#states[M1-STATE-2,
M1-STATE-3, and M1-STATE-5], plus
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/442[#442] through

https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/454[#454], packaged evidence
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/459[#459], and integrated

|8. Dependency conformance
|A source-dependency edge, concrete mechanism dependency or value use, or
core-owned contract governed by accepted ADR 0001, ADR 0003, or ADR 0004 is
added or changed.
|Mapped M1 source graph and shell boundaries. TypeScript language use, runtime
calls, messages, data flow, and ADR 0002 placement are considered separately
from source direction and concrete mechanism dependence.
|The cross-stratum graph contains only `edge→application`,
capability-authorized `edge→domain`, and `application→domain`. The named
worker-pipeline coordinator→worker-stage application-capability dependency is
permitted. Out-of-capability edge-to-domain access and every named forbidden
dependency are rejected. Application core and domain core import or depend on
no Vite, UI-framework, or concrete DOM/Worker mechanism module, instantiate or
otherwise depend on no concrete DOM/Worker mechanism value, and expose no
DOM/Worker mechanism type in a core-owned contract. No general same-stratum
rule is inferred.
|#452's initial automated evidence accepts representative
`edge→application`, capability-authorized `edge→domain`, and
`application→domain` cross-stratum dependencies plus
`worker-pipeline coordinator→named worker-stage application capability`. It
rejects representative out-of-capability `edge→domain`, `application→edge`,
`domain→edge`, `domain→application`, UI↔worker implementation, and
`stage capability→predecessor-stage implementation` dependencies. For both
application core and domain core, it also rejects representative imports or
dependencies on Vite, a UI framework, and concrete DOM/Worker mechanisms,
concrete DOM/Worker mechanism value dependence or instantiation, and
DOM/Worker mechanism types in core-owned contracts. #460 executes the complete
check against its final head after all M1 dependency and build-input changes.
|xref:adr/0001-core-dependency-boundary.adoc[ADR 0001],
xref:adr/0003-m1-core-strata-building-blocks-and-conceptual-ports.adoc[ADR 0003],
and xref:adr/0004-framework-free-typescript-shell-built-with-vite.adoc[ADR 0004]
are the accepted source-conformance rules. Initial automated evidence belongs
to https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/452[#452]; final-head
execution belongs to https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

renewal/stop rules. #454 owns controlled mechanism evidence, #459 the packaged
successful path, and #460 hosted final-head renewal. Both ADRs' dated
observations are no warranty, and this documentation is not evidence that
product traffic exists.

The broader ADR 0004 shell and artifact evidence has distinct future owners.
#452 owns the initial walking shell, actual committed dependency closure,
license and notice metadata and applicable notices, current point-in-time
vulnerability results, integrity metadata, byte-identical artifact-tree
comparison, `/code-city/` base, separate same-origin ES-module worker, and
exclusion evidence. https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/459[#459]
owns successful integration through that package. #460 owns canonical final
production renewal of the closure, license and notice metadata and applicable
notices, current vulnerability results, integrity, source-conformance,
artifact-tree, base-path, separate-worker, and exclusion evidence after every
M1 dependency and build-input change.
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/464[#464] verifies ADR 0004
acceptance, those evidence owners, and their readiness checks before M1
implementation handoff. This documentation is not implementation evidence and
does not claim that the shell or artifact exists now.

RA-10 retrieval duration and RA-11 admission duration are no longer deferred:
they are explicitly unnecessary and have no value, unit, timer, clock,
category, code, interval, cutoff, boundary, or precedence. Scenario 6 has no
elapsed-time target, and arbitrary time alone may leave an attempt `Loading`
without creating an outcome. SP-01 through SP-04, CC-09, and PR-01 remain
deferred to their linked owners in
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#deferred-limits[RA timing
disposition and downstream limit decisions].
