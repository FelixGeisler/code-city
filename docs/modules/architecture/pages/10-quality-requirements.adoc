= 10. Quality Requirements

The matrix below is the closed M1 quality-scenario set. Numerical limits and
detailed contracts are linked to their normative requirements rather than
copied. No elapsed-time target, tool-specific test prescription, or screenshot
equality is introduced.

[cols="2,5,4,6,6,6",options="header"]
|===
|Scenario |Stimulus |Environment |Required response |Objective evidence |Traceability

|1. Immutable pinning
|GitHub's designated default-branch tip moves after the current attempt selected
a revision; the user may later resubmit.
|Current attempt is loading or complete, followed by a separate later
submission.
|The current attempt remains pinned to its full selected commit ID; only the
later attempt may select the newer tip.
|Controlled resolution evidence fixes the first handoff despite branch movement
and permits a new handoff only after resubmission.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/441[#441]
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#revision-selection[M1-REV-1]
and
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#immutable-revision[M1-REV-2];
evidence https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/453[#453] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/459[#459].

|2. Canonical repeatability
|The same selected identity and equivalent bounded repository data are
processed repeatedly while provider or carrier order, locale, time,
concurrency, or device order varies.
|Successful accepted M1 journey.
|Admission result, canonical metric facts, and canonical scene facts are
identical. Exact equality applies except only the camera-evaluation tolerance
accepted in #444; screenshots are not evidence.
|Permutation and repetition evidence compares the canonical admitted-module
sequence, metric facts, scene facts, and semantic presentation result.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/442[#442]
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#admission-composition[M1-ADM-5]
and
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#outcome-precedence[M1-OUT-2];
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/443[#443]
xref:requirements:08-fixed-metric-semantics.adoc#canonical-identities[canonical
identities/order] and
xref:requirements:08-fixed-metric-semantics.adoc#canonical-facts[canonical
facts]; https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/444[#444]
xref:requirements:10-fixed-city-layout-and-presentation.adoc#precision-repeatability[precision
and repeatability]. Evidence:
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/454[#454],

|3. Anonymous operation
|The user submits invalid input or an accepted URL whose repository is missing,
private, access denied, or otherwise anonymously unavailable.
|Fresh or replacement attempt without credentials.
|Invalid input causes no provider interaction. Accepted input sends no user or
service credential. Unavailable cases remain indistinguishable and never offer
authentication recovery.
|Boundary evidence proves no interaction for invalid input, no authorization
material for accepted interactions, and one privacy-preserving unavailable
outcome.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/441[#441]
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#url-grammar[M1-URL-1],
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#anonymous-access[M1-ANON-1
and M1-ANON-2], and
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#failure-outcomes[M1-FAIL-1];
evidence https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/453[#453],
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/454[#454],
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/459[#459], and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|4. No source execution
|Adversarial metadata or source is received, including a side-effect sentinel
or malformed supported-family syntax.
|Admission, static metric processing, and presentation of the current attempt.
|Every value remains inert data: no execution, evaluation, import, or dynamic
loading occurs. Malformed syntax produces `M1-MET-1`; repository-derived model
identities remain inert and never become markup or resource locators.
|Adversarial fixtures observe no sentinel side effect or active interpretation
and verify the exact atomic malformed-source outcome.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/442[#442]
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#untrusted-source[M1-ADM-0];
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/443[#443]
xref:requirements:08-fixed-metric-semantics.adoc#metric-input[input boundary]
and
xref:requirements:08-fixed-metric-semantics.adoc#metric-failure[M1-MET-1]; and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/444[#444]
xref:requirements:10-fixed-city-layout-and-presentation.adoc#canonical-scene[canonical-scene
exclusions]. Evidence: https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/454[#454],
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/455[#455],
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/458[#458], and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|5. Bounded admission
|Actual repository or intake data reaches T-1, T, or T+1 for any RA-01 through
RA-09 dimension, or combines or permutes cutoff and failure conditions; valid
metric input reaches city construction.
|Current attempt, with equivalent provider orders and untrusted measurement
claims.
|The exact horizon, inclusive-boundary, precedence, and category/code result
from #442 applies to actual data; no partial admitted set is published. Valid
city input follows #444's CC-01 through CC-08 dispositions without a private
cap.
|Closed boundary, cutoff, precedence, and permutation evidence covers every RA
row; city evidence covers the fixed CC dispositions and integrated failure
state.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/442[#442]
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#limit-rule[M1-LIM-0],
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#metadata-policy[M1-LIM-1],
and
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#failure-categories[M1-OUT-1]
through
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#failure-invariants[M1-OUT-3],
plus https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/444[#444]
xref:requirements:10-fixed-city-layout-and-presentation.adoc#city-limit-dispositions[CC
dispositions]; evidence https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/454[#454],
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/457[#457], and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|6. Cancellation
|The user cancels `Loading`, including when cancellation and a result are tied.
|Current attempt may or may not already have a selected identity; the input
draft may have been edited.
|The exact `Cancelled` state is observable: draft preserved, identity and city
absent, and every late outcome rejected. No elapsed-time target or physical
cleanup mechanism is defined.
|A controlled race or tie scenario proves cancellation wins observable
publication and late results change nothing.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/441[#441]
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#states[M1-STATE-4 and
M1-STATE-5] and the downstream atomicity invariants; integrated evidence
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|7. Replacement and atomic publication
|A user submits again while any prior state or attempt exists, and the
replacement succeeds or fails at any stage.
|Current session with a prior loading, success, failure, or cancelled state.
|The new submission invalidates prior work before validation; only the current
attempt can publish. Failure shows no stale or partial city; success publishes
the complete city and identity together.
|End-to-end replacement, late-result, stage-failure, and
successful-publication evidence proves one current outcome and no partial or
stale output.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/441[#441]
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#states[M1-STATE-2,
M1-STATE-3, and M1-STATE-5], plus
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/442[#442]–https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/444[#444]
atomic failures; evidence https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/459[#459]
and https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|8. Dependency conformance
|A source-dependency edge is added or changed after #445 has produced exactly
one accepted core-dependency ADR.
|Packaged M1 source graph; runtime calls and data flow are considered
separately.
|The graph conforms to that accepted ADR; runtime direction is not treated as
source-dependency direction.
|#452's automated evidence accepts a conforming graph and rejects one
representative forbidden edge.
|The decision in https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/445[#445] is
*deferred/not accepted here*. Conditional evidence belongs to
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/452[#452].
|===
