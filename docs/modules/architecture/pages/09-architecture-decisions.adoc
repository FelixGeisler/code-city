* xref:adr/0008-browser-native-webgl2-instanced-city-presentation.adoc[ADR 0008: Browser-native WebGL2 instanced city presentation]
  is `Accepted`. https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/451[#451]
  records the clear Software Architect recommendation, incorporated Security
  Reviewer constraints, and Requirements Engineer acceptance under #444's
  human delegation for accepted issue-body SHA-256
  `617c8eb9ff5671ca399eb3ed4f0656e76feb06c211fe880bdb399011826d3126`.
  The decision needs no later generic human-acceptance gate. It keeps city
  construction renderer-neutral, confines fixed raw WebGL2 to the UI-main
  presentation edge, resolves CC-09/PR-01 as unnecessary, and fixes source-
  free projection, failure ownership, resources, shaders, context, API/state,
  camera/float proof, atomic staging, resize, context loss, cleanup, evidence,
  and stop rules. #452 remains renderer-free, #457 owns canonical construction
  and float-bound renewal, #458 owns all renderer realization, #459 integrates,
  #460 renews final hosted evidence, and #463/#464 own synthesis/readiness.
* xref:adr/0009-m1-main-line-and-github-pages-cutover.adoc[ADR 0009: M1 main-line and GitHub Pages cutover]
  is `Accepted`. https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/446[#446]
  records the direct 2026-08-17 human branch and documentation decision and
  accepts it under issue-body SHA-256
  `1a4c1159dbfb15f03e2419ebf52124b697e22966a67de91ba8fe60ac0a36233d`.
  ADR 0009 complements rather than supersedes ADR 0002: it fixes the
  identity-preserving `v2`-to-`main` and v1-`main`-to-locked-`v1` transition,
  sole new-main Pages authority, archival documentation disposition, and
  app-over-docs cutover. It is a publication and repository-governance
  decision, not a successor to ADR 0001's core-dependency boundary. #464 owns
  the separately evidenced administration gate, #452 owns non-deploying exact
  workflow realization, and #460 owns the guarded final deployment and exact-
  origin evidence.
