= 3. Context and Scope

This view describes logical roles and exchanges only. It assigns no internal
owner or mechanism.

== Local terms

logical system boundary:: The topology- and technology-neutral boundary around
all accepted Code City behavior. It states what is inside the system, not where
or how that behavior runs.

logical trust crossing:: An inbound handoff from an external role to Code City
at which the received values remain untrusted. It is not a physical trust zone
or deployment boundary.

delivery-target role:: The environmental role through which Code City is
opened and presented. It is not a selected deployment node or trust zone.

== Logical context

[source,text]
----
+--------+       accepted user journey       +=======================+       two distinct exchanges       +---------------+
|  User  | <-------------------------------> ||      Code City      || <-------------------------------> | Public GitHub |
+--------+                                    || logical system       ||                                   +---------------+
                                              || boundary             ||
                                              +=======================+
                                                          :
                                                          : opened and presented through
                                            . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
                                            .    delivery-target role     .
                                            . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
----

The arrows denote logical interactions, not processes, ports, APIs, runtime
calls, network routes, or physical placement. The dotted environmental role is
not another system or trust zone.

== Logical trust crossings

[cols="3,5,7",options="header"]
|===
|Crossing |Inbound values |Trust and authority

|User to Code City
|The URL draft and submitted value.
|All inbound data remains untrusted, and no user or service credential crosses
the boundary. See
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#anonymous-access[Anonymous
public access] and
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#untrusted-source[M1-ADM-0];
evidence https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/453[#453] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|Public GitHub to Code City
|Resolution evidence, repository metadata, paths, entry content, and source.
|All inbound data remains untrusted, and no credential crosses the boundary.
GitHub is authoritative only for canonical repository spelling and its
designated default-branch tip, subject to later accepted identity validation.
See xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#url-normalization[M1-URL-2],
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#revision-selection[M1-REV-1],
and
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#untrusted-source[M1-ADM-0].
Identity validation remains *deferred/not accepted here* in
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/448[#448]; evidence belongs to
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/453[#453] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/454[#454].
|===

These are the only logical trust crossings. The delivery-target role creates no
additional crossing in this view, and no credential crosses any boundary.
Runtime trust placement remains *deferred/not accepted here* in
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/446[#446].

== Data lifecycle

current-session state:: The logical product state retained only for the open
session and reset by reload.

processing data:: Inert data used while producing the current attempt's
outcome; it is neither a persisted product/history record nor user output.

[cols="3,7,5",options="header"]
|===
|Data class |Contents and lifecycle |Contract and evidence

|Prohibited
|User and service credentials are never requested, accepted, read, retained, or
transmitted.
|xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#anonymous-access[Anonymous
public access]; evidence https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/453[#453]
and https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|Current-session state
|Input draft, stable state and status, the selected repository/revision tuple
where permitted, and the complete city only in `Success`. Submission,
replacement, cancellation, failure, success, and reload change or clear these
exactly as the accepted state contract specifies.
|xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#states[M1-STATE-1 through
M1-STATE-5] and
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#_m1_ex_1_excluded_controls_and_retained_data[M1-EX-1];
evidence https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/459[#459] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|Processing data
|Branch designation and evidence, provider records, paths, raw and normalized
source, admitted modules, canonical metric facts, limit counters, and
city-construction intermediates. They are neither persisted/history records
nor user output. Their physical lifetime and cleanup mechanism remain
deferred.
|xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#_m1_ex_1_excluded_controls_and_retained_data[M1-EX-1],
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#admission-composition[M1-ADM-5],
xref:requirements:08-fixed-metric-semantics.adoc#canonical-facts[Canonical
metric facts], and
xref:requirements:10-fixed-city-layout-and-presentation.adoc#canonical-scene[Canonical
scene facts]; stage evidence
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/453[#453] through
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/458[#458]. Physical lifecycle
and cleanup remain *deferred/not accepted here* in
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/446[#446].
|===

No secure-erasure promise, cleanup latency, or physical cleanup behavior is
accepted here. Runtime topology and placement are deferred to
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/446[#446], provider and request
mechanisms to https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/448[#448] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/449[#449], and internal
responsibilities and ports to
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/462[#462].
