= 2. Architecture Constraints

The following set is closed for this baseline. Each constraint links to its
normative requirement instead of copying detailed grammar, matrices, formulas,
precedence tables, or numerical thresholds.

[cols="5,6,4",options="header"]
|===
|Accepted constraint |Normative source |Evidence owner

|One canonical public-GitHub URL is the sole user input. Each syntactically
accepted current attempt selects and identifies one immutable selected
revision at the GitHub-designated default-branch tip.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/441[#441]:
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#canonical-url[Canonical URL
input] and
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#immutable-revision[Immutable
revision selection and identity].
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/453[#453] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/459[#459].

|Operation is anonymous. User and service credentials are prohibited, and
anonymously unavailable conditions preserve the accepted privacy behavior.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/441[#441]:
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#anonymous-access[Anonymous
public access] and
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#failure-outcomes[Required
failure categories].
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/453[#453] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|User values, GitHub values, repository metadata, paths, entry content, and
source remain untrusted data. Repository data is never executed, evaluated,
imported, or dynamically loaded.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/442[#442]:
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#untrusted-source[M1-ADM-0];
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/443[#443]:
xref:requirements:08-fixed-metric-semantics.adoc#metric-input[Input boundary];
and https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/444[#444]:
xref:requirements:10-fixed-city-layout-and-presentation.adoc#canonical-scene[Canonical
scene and exclusions].
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/454[#454],
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/455[#455],
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/458[#458], and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|The accepted admission and ordering rules and RA-01 through RA-09, canonical
metric facts and metric failure, and city, scene, presentation, and CC-01
through CC-08 dispositions are exact and non-configurable.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/442[#442]:
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#admission-composition[M1-ADM-5],
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#limit-rule[M1-LIM-0], and
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#outcome-precedence[M1-OUT-2];
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/443[#443]:
xref:requirements:08-fixed-metric-semantics.adoc#canonical-facts[Canonical
facts] and
xref:requirements:08-fixed-metric-semantics.adoc#metric-failure[Atomic metric
failure]; https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/444[#444]:
xref:requirements:10-fixed-city-layout-and-presentation.adoc#canonical-scene[Canonical
scene],
xref:requirements:10-fixed-city-layout-and-presentation.adoc#city-limit-dispositions[CC
dispositions], and
xref:requirements:10-fixed-city-layout-and-presentation.adoc#city-failure[stage
failure].
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/454[#454] through
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|Submission, replacement, cancellation, failure, success, identity retention,
and complete-city publication obey the current-attempt invariant: no stale,
partial, or late result may be published.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/441[#441]:
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#states[M1-STATE-1 through
M1-STATE-5]; https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/442[#442]:
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#failure-invariants[M1-OUT-3];
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/443[#443]:
xref:requirements:08-fixed-metric-semantics.adoc#metric-failure[Atomic metric
failure]; and https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/444[#444]:
xref:requirements:10-fixed-city-layout-and-presentation.adoc#city-failure[Atomic
construction and presentation failure].
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/459[#459] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|Product state is current-session only and reload resets it. M1 creates no
persisted product or history record; repository payloads and derived
intermediates are not persisted product records.
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/441[#441]:
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#states[Observable states and
transitions] and
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#_m1_ex_1_excluded_controls_and_retained_data[M1-EX-1];
xref:requirements:01-product-scope.adoc#non-goals[Non-goals].
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/459[#459] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|M1 has no authentication or other input, configuration, exploration, history,
persistence, export, compatibility promise, or speculative extension point.
|xref:requirements:01-product-scope.adoc#non-goals[Non-goals] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/441[#441]
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#_m1_ex_1_excluded_controls_and_retained_data[M1-EX-1].
|https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/459[#459] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].
|===

xref:adr/0001-core-dependency-boundary.adoc[ADR 0001] is the accepted
source-dependency constraint. Topology, internal dependency direction and
ownership, delivery, provider and request mechanisms, analysis, visualization,
enforcement, and physical cleanup remain *deferred/not accepted here*. Their
owners are https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/446[#446] through
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/451[#451] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/462[#462]; none of those open
choices is an accepted constraint on this page.
