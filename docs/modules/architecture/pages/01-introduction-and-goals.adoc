= 1. Introduction and Goals

== M1 outcome

The M1 architecture serves one accepted outcome: a user opens the approved
Code City delivery target, submits one canonical URL for an anonymously
accessible public GitHub repository, and receives one deterministic,
non-configurable city for supported JavaScript and TypeScript modules from one
immutable selected revision. The normative outcome and its exclusions remain in
xref:requirements:01-product-scope.adoc#_m1_outcome[M1 outcome] and
xref:requirements:01-product-scope.adoc#non-goals[Non-goals]; this page does not
redefine them.

== Prioritized architecture drivers

Every accepted requirement in
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/441[#441],
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/442[#442],
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/443[#443], and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/444[#444] is a mandatory gate.
The following priority applies only when alternatives conform equally to all
of those requirements. It is a reversible documentation synthesis made by the
Requirements Engineer under the human delegation recorded in
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/461[#461]; it introduces no
numerical promise or architecture or technology selection.

[cols="1,4,7",options="header"]
|===

|Preserve the exact immutable identity, deterministic sequence of admitted
modules, canonical metric facts, canonical scene facts, and semantic
repeatability defined by
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#immutable-revision[Immutable
revision selection and identity],
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#admission-composition[M1-ADM-5],

== Explicit trade-offs

[cols="4,6,5",options="header"]
|===
|Prefer |Consequence |Traceability

|Exact canonical results over heuristics or fallback
|Do not repair input, select a fallback revision, approximate metric facts, or
substitute a layout, palette, camera, or prior city.
|xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#revision-selection[M1-REV-1],
xref:requirements:08-fixed-metric-semantics.adoc#canonical-facts[Canonical
metric facts], and
xref:requirements:10-fixed-city-layout-and-presentation.adoc#canonical-scene[Canonical
scene facts]; evidence
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/453[#453],
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/455[#455] through
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/459[#459].

|Bounded inspection over exhaustive diagnostics
|Stop at the accepted inspection horizon and classify only established facts;
do not inspect excluded data to seek another diagnostic.
|xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#admission-composition[Bounded
classification] and
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#outcome-precedence[Deterministic
precedence]; evidence https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/454[#454].

|Current-attempt atomicity over progressive or stale display
|Only the current attempt may publish, and it publishes the complete city and
identity together or no city at all.
|xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#states[M1-STATE-1 through
M1-STATE-5],
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#failure-invariants[M1-OUT-3],
and
xref:requirements:10-fixed-city-layout-and-presentation.adoc#city-failure[Atomic
construction and presentation failure]; evidence
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/459[#459] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|Anonymous non-executing processing over broader repository access
|Accept no credential and never execute, evaluate, import, or dynamically load
repository metadata or source.
|xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#anonymous-access[Anonymous
public access] and
xref:requirements:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#untrusted-source[M1-ADM-0];
evidence https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/453[#453],
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/454[#454], and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].

|Accepted dependency rules over avoiding translation cost
|Accept translation work when an accepted dependency decision requires it; do
not weaken an accepted decision or pre-accept a deferred one for implementation
convenience.
|The delegated synthesis is recorded in
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/461[#461].
xref:adr/0001-core-dependency-boundary.adoc[ADR 0001] is accepted through
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/445[#445], with automated
evidence in https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/452[#452].

|M1 scope over extensibility
|Add no authentication, configuration, exploration, persistence, compatibility
layer, generic extension point, or other capability outside the one journey.
|xref:requirements:01-product-scope.adoc#non-goals[Non-goals] and
xref:requirements:04-functional-requirements.adoc#_m1_ex_1_excluded_controls_and_retained_data[M1-EX-1];
evidence https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/459[#459] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/460[#460].
|===

== Decision boundary

The core-dependency choice owned by
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/445[#445] is accepted in
xref:adr/0001-core-dependency-boundary.adoc[ADR 0001]. The choices owned by
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/446[#446],
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/447[#447],
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/448[#448],

https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/462[#462] remain
*deferred/not accepted here*. In particular, this page selects no topology,
deployment target or node, process, component, port, API or provider mechanism,
origin/request/redirect policy, shell, analyzer, renderer, enforcement
location, technology, or physical cleanup mechanism.

https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/463[#463] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/464[#464] are later synthesis
and fitness consumers only. `npm run docs:build` validates this documentation;
it is not an M1 runtime-technology constraint.
