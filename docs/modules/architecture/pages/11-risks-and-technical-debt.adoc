= 11. Risks and Technical Debt

This is the closed M1 risk register. It links each risk to already accepted
requirements or decisions and allocates observation, response, evidence,
renewal, and reopening; it does not introduce severity, likelihood, deadline,
workaround, or future design.

== Technical-debt boundary

No known technical-debt item authorizes a TODO, waiver, lower private capacity,
stale evidence, or design to be selected later by a Developer. Unimplemented
M1 work is owned backlog, not accepted debt. A discovered conformance gap
blocks its owning issue and is returned to the linked requirement or ADR; it is
not silently entered here as debt.
