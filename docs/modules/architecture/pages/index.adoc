= Architecture

This arc42 module records the accepted M0 architecture baseline for M1. The
repository-administration prerequisite is complete, while the M1 package,
executable M1 build/Pages workflow, product traffic, parser, renderer, and
application deployment remain unimplemented. Accepted decisions are constraints on later
issues, not claims that their mechanisms already exist.

== Current baseline

The sealed administration baseline records protected/default `main` at
`b0b1a0a4e873b9415d266b67c7adeda913faf655`, the former v1 line as locked
archival `v1` at `3ed8407809ea660657d6eeb49579ddb8412932fa`, and no `v2`
branch. ADR 0009 administration is sealed under SHA-256
`bc8df73542bf11dc8d09898e09764d93f1a40ebcf195b8002d594b201d1b05bb`.
The summarized current state and the still-frozen legacy Pages deployment are
indexed in xref:07-deployment-view.adoc[Deployment View]; no raw private
administration evidence belongs in this documentation.

xref:adr/0001-core-dependency-boundary.adoc[ADR 0001] is the sole core
source-dependency ADR and has no accepted successor.
xref:adr/0003-m1-core-strata-building-blocks-and-conceptual-ports.adoc[ADR 0003]
is an internal refinement, not a successor. All nine decision ADRs are
`Accepted`; xref:09-architecture-decisions.adoc[Architecture Decisions] records
the status and authority closure without duplicating their contents.

== Reader entry points

* Requirements readers begin with xref:requirements:index.adoc[the normative
  requirements reading order], then use
  xref:01-introduction-and-goals.adoc[Introduction and Goals] for architecture
  drivers.
* Implementers use xref:04-solution-strategy.adoc[Solution Strategy],
  xref:05-building-block-view.adoc[Building Block View], and
  xref:06-runtime-view.adoc[Runtime View], then follow the sole-owner and
  evidence allocations in
  xref:08-crosscutting-concepts.adoc[Cross-cutting Concepts].
* Issue-readiness reviewers use
  xref:10-quality-requirements.adoc[Quality Requirements] for the closed
  scenario and issue-fitness allocations. Every M1 issue still requires its own
  same-digest readiness review before Developer handoff.
* Architecture reviewers use
  xref:09-architecture-decisions.adoc[Architecture Decisions],
  xref:11-risks-and-technical-debt.adoc[Risks and Technical Debt], and
  xref:12-glossary.adoc[Glossary] as indexes to the governing requirements,
  views, ADRs, evidence owners, and reopen owners.
* Deployment and publication reviewers use
  xref:07-deployment-view.adoc#accepted-current-deferred[Accepted, current, and
  still deferred] to distinguish completed administration, accepted
  publication policy, unimplemented M1 work, and issue-owned local detail.
