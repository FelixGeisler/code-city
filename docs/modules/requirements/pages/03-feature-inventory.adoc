= Feature Inventory

This inventory classifies M1 capabilities without redefining their contracts.
The xref:01-product-scope.adoc#non-goals[Product Scope non-goals] are
authoritative for exclusions.

[cols="2,1,4",options="header"]
|===
|Capability |M1 status |Contract or owner

|Single canonical public GitHub repository URL
|Included
|Exact grammar and normalization are in
xref:04-functional-requirements.adoc#canonical-url[Canonical URL input].

|Anonymous public-repository access
|Included
|No user or service credentials; private and non-public repositories are
excluded by xref:04-functional-requirements.adoc#anonymous-access[Anonymous
public access].

|Automatic immutable revision
|Included
|The default branch tip is selected once and identified by the full commit ID;
see xref:04-functional-requirements.adoc#immutable-revision[Immutable revision
selection and identity].

|Initial, loading, success, failure, replacement, and cancellation behavior
|Included
|The state machine and no-stale/no-partial invariant are in
xref:04-functional-requirements.adoc#states[Observable states and transitions].

|Selected-revision retrieval, supported-source admission, and repository/intake limits
|Included; accepted contract and ADR-owned capability
|xref:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc[] defines the closed module-admission
matrix, approved RA-01 through RA-09 maxima, deterministic classification, and
observable category/code pairs under
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/442[#442].
xref:architecture:adr/0006-bounded-selected-revision-retrieval-via-github-tree-and-raw-content.adoc[ADR 0006]
owns bounded retrieval of the immutable selected revision and the sole complete
ordered admitted-module set. RA-10 retrieval duration and RA-11 admission
duration are explicitly unnecessary for M1 and have no timer, value, boundary,
or outcome. SP-01 through SP-04 are likewise explicitly unnecessary: all
RA-07/RA-08/RA-09-admitted work reaches analysis and no static-processing timer
or lower processing limit exists. CC-09 and PR-01 are explicitly unnecessary:
neither construction nor presentation has a timer, elapsed-time outcome, or
lower private limit. CC-01 through CC-08 retain the fixed #444 dispositions.

|Fixed metric semantics
|Included; accepted contract
|xref:08-fixed-metric-semantics.adoc[] defines lexical physical-line SLOC,
executable units, per-unit and maximum complexity, canonical facts, the exact
suffix-to-grammar behavior, recovered-tree rejection, and atomic metric failure
under https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/443[#443] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/450[#450].
xref:architecture:adr/0007-static-syntax-with-pinned-tree-sitter-webassembly-grammars.adoc[ADR 0007]
fixes the non-executing Tree-sitter mechanism, primitive projection, and
phase-aware lifecycle. Analysis responsibility remains with the accepted core
and port owners; city mapping is fixed by the next contract.

|Fixed city, layout, and presentation
|Included; accepted contract and ADR-owned mechanism
|xref:10-fixed-city-layout-and-presentation.adoc[] defines exact prism mapping,
palette, scene exclusions, unsigned-UTF-8 square shelves and bounds,
orthographic framing, no interaction, precision, all CC/PR dispositions, the
`M1-CITY-1`/`M1-PRES-1` boundary, and post-`Success` context-loss/resize-failure
behavior under https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/444[#444] and
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/451[#451].
xref:architecture:adr/0008-browser-native-webgl2-instanced-city-presentation.adoc[ADR
0008] accepts the fixed browser-native WebGL2 UI-edge realization, including
source-free projection, one instanced draw, passive resize, atomic staging, and
cleanup.

|Authentication, private repositories, or credentials
|Excluded
|M1 accepts neither credential input nor authenticated operation.

|Other repository inputs or revision controls
|Excluded
|There is no non-GitHub/local/archive input and no branch, tag, commit, or
second-input control.

|Configuration, exploration, history, persistence, or export
|Excluded
|These capability families are listed in the authoritative
xref:01-product-scope.adoc#non-goals[non-goals].

|v1 compatibility or feature parity
|Excluded
|Code City v1 is comparison evidence only, not the M1 specification.
|===

Architecture choices are not product features in this inventory. Their owners
are linked from xref:04-functional-requirements.adoc#decision-boundary[Decision
boundary], and no choice is implied by an `Included` row above.
