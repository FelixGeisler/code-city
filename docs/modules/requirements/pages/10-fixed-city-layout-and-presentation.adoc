= Fixed City Layout and Presentation

This page is the authoritative M1 product contract that turns a complete
successful canonical module-metric sequence into one deterministic city and
keeps its fixed presentation valid. It applies only after
xref:08-fixed-metric-semantics.adoc[] has produced the complete canonical facts
for the admitted modules.

The renderer-neutral canonical city facts remain those accepted in #444.
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/451[#451] additionally fixes
the source-free construction/presentation boundary, passive-resize and
post-`Success` failure behavior, and explicitly-unnecessary CC-09/PR-01
disposition on this page. The browser-native WebGL2 edge realization is
accepted separately in
xref:architecture:adr/0008-browser-native-webgl2-instanced-city-presentation.adoc[ADR
0008]; renderer implementation facts do not become canonical scene facts.
Static syntax remains independently fixed by
xref:architecture:adr/0007-static-syntax-with-pinned-tree-sitter-webassembly-grammars.adoc[ADR
0007].

[[city-terms]]
== Terms

City unit:: The abstract exact unit used for building dimensions, positions,
gaps, and bounds. It is not a device pixel or a renderer-specific unit.

Building identity:: Exactly the module's NFC canonical path from
xref:06-source-admission-and-limits.adoc#canonical-paths[M1-ADM-1].

Canonical scene facts:: Only the successful facts listed under
<<canonical-scene,Canonical scene and exclusions>>. Renderer implementation
facts are not added to this set.

Initial-view descriptor:: The renderer-neutral orthographic target, basis,
projection extents, and fit rule under <<initial-view,Fixed initial view and
interaction>>.

Valid complete canonical model:: The one complete, internally consistent
canonical scene authoritatively validated and constructed by the worker-side
city constructor for the current eligible generation.

Current published presentation:: The one canvas and fixed WebGL2 resources
that currently realize a valid complete canonical model in `Success`.

[[city-input]]
== Input boundary

The city stage consumes only the complete successful canonical module sequence
defined by xref:08-fixed-metric-semantics.adoc#canonical-facts[]. For each
module, `S` is SLOC, `U` is executable-unit count, and `M` is numeric maximum
complexity. Module identity and those three values remain exactly the facts
defined by #442 and #443; this stage does not rename, normalize, clamp, or
recalculate them.

For `N >= 1`, one and only one building must correspond to each input identity.
A duplicate identity or invalid #443 fact, or any missing, additional, or
repeated model mapping, fails construction under <<city-failure,Atomic
construction and presentation failure>>. The worker-side city constructor is
the authoritative owner of that validation and complete construction.

[[presentation-boundary]]
== Construction and source-free presentation boundary

The UI presentation adapter consumes only one valid complete canonical model
for the current eligible generation. It receives no repository source,
admitted modules, parser or analyzer objects, metric intermediates, or partial
scene. Before any WebGL resource is created, it defensively validates and
projects the complete handoff field by field:

* every required root, collection, building, vector, color, bounds, and view
  field is an own data property with the expected primitive or bounded
  collection value;
* missing or inherited required values, accessor-backed expected fields,
  sparse or wrong-count collections, wrong primitive types, non-finite values,
  unsafe integers, duplicate identities, inconsistent geometry, bounds,
  palette, order, view, or unrepresentable canonical values are construction-
  contract breaches;
* additional own fields outside the canonical contract are ignored without
  evaluating their values or accessors merely for diagnostics, becoming
  control state, or permitting an additional canonical mapping or collection
  entry;
* `N` equals the complete building count, identities are unique, and
  `1 <= N <= 4,000`; integer geometry and palette bytes are exact; every
  #444 layout, bounds, order, palette, target, basis, projection, and no-
  interaction fact agrees, with only #444's camera-scalar tolerance;
* arrays and `Map`, or an equivalent prototype-safe map, may carry collections;
  a building identity never becomes a property key in a normal object; and
* a throwing descriptor or validation operation is a construction-contract
  breach, not a raw exception or presentation diagnostic.

The adapter never repairs, reorders, clamps, truncates, reconstructs, or
reclassifies malformed canonical data. A detected model defect or validation
throw creates no WebGL resource, cleans local staging, and propagates only
`City construction failed` / `M1-CITY-1`. The bridge transports the fixed
outcome without classifying exceptions. The UI attempt coordinator alone
commits current visible state.

Only after the complete model passes that defensive validation can canvas,
context, GPU-representation, allocation, shader/program, upload, draw, framing,
passive-resize, or context-loss failure produce `Presentation failed` /
`M1-PRES-1`. Building paths and identities, repository/source values, URLs,
object keys, markup, and diagnostics never become DOM text, names, IDs,
classes, attributes, WebGL labels, shader text or defines, resource locators,
or log content. The separately displayed canonical repository URL and full
commit ID remain outside the adapter.

[[building-geometry]]
== Coordinate system and building geometry

Use a right-handed coordinate system with the X/Z ground plane and positive Y
vertical. Each module produces one axis-aligned, unrotated, closed rectangular
prism resting at `y = 0`.

Its dimensions in exact city units are:

[source,text]
----
height = S + 1
width = depth = U + 1
----

Consequently `(S,U) = (0,0)` produces a visible `1 × 1 × 1` prism, and every
later integer value remains distinct. No scaling, normalization, logarithm,
minimum or maximum clamp, repository-relative adjustment, or geometry rounding
is permitted.

[[fixed-palette]]
== Fixed opaque sRGB palette

Apply the following RGBA byte value to every face. Do not interpolate or apply
repository-relative normalization.

[cols="3,3",options="header"]
|===
|Maximum complexity `M` |RGBA

|`0` |`#440154FF`
|`1` |`#414487FF`
|`2–3` |`#2A788EFF`
|`4–7` |`#22A884FF`
|`8–15` |`#7AD151FF`
|`16+` |`#FDE725FF`
|===

The final band saturates only displayed color. The canonical maximum-complexity
fact remains unclamped.

[[canonical-scene]]
== Canonical scene and exclusions

A building identity is an inert model key. It is never display text, markup, a
URL, a material or shader name, or a resource locator. The canonical repository
URL and full lowercase commit ID required by
xref:04-functional-requirements.adoc#immutable-revision[] remain outside the
city scene.

Canonical scene facts are exactly:

. the ordered buildings, each with its identity, minimum corner, dimensions,
  and palette RGBA;
. the city axis-aligned bounds;
. the initial-view descriptor under <<initial-view>>; and
. the fixed no-interaction policy.

M1 has no building labels, tooltips, legend, ground plane, roads, districts,
hierarchy, decorative geometry, or unit-level objects. Renderer meshes,
triangles, draw calls, lighting, background, antialiasing, device color
management, and camera implementation objects are not canonical facts.

[[square-shelf-layout]]
== Unsigned-UTF-8 square-shelf layout and bounds

Disregard carrier iteration order and sort the `N` unique building identities
in #442 ascending unsigned-UTF-8 order. Let `C` be the smallest positive integer
for which `C^2 >= N`. For zero-based building index `i`, let:

[source,text]
----
r = floor(i/C)
k = i mod C
b_i = U_i + 1
----

Place buildings row-major with `C` slots per row and a fixed edge-to-edge gap
`g = 1` city unit. For row `r`, its present indices are:

[source,text]
----
J_r = {rC, ..., min(N,(r+1)C)-1}
D_r = max(b_j) for j in J_r
----

The minimum corner of building `i` is calculated exactly as:

[source,text]
----
x_i = sum(b_j + g) for j = rC through i-1
z_i = sum(D_q + g) for q = 0 through r-1
minimum corner_i = (x_i, 0, z_i)
----

An empty sum is `0`. The first building starts at the world origin, and rows
advance in positive Z. The one-unit gap and each preceding row's maximum depth
prevent overlap for unequal footprints. No size sort, packing heuristic,
hierarchy, or repository-dependent placement may replace this order or layout.

The city axis-aligned bounds are exactly:

[source,text]
----
minimum = (0, 0, 0)
maximum = (max_i(x_i + b_i), max_i(S_i + 1), max_i(z_i + b_i))
----

CC-01 through CC-08 create no independent limit-exceeded outcome. Missing,
additional, or repeated mappings and malformed or unrepresentable facts use
`M1-CITY-1` under <<city-failure>>. A private implementation capacity cannot
create an undeclared product limit.

Arbitrary elapsed construction or presentation time alone creates no outcome;
while work has produced neither a browser/platform failure nor a lifecycle
result, the attempt remains `Loading`. M1 makes no completion, startup, frame-
time, throughput, latency, or preemption promise. Complete-envelope bounds,
fixed resources, `O(N)` projection/upload for `N <= 4,000`, one draw call, and
the accepted float proof establish feasibility instead of a timer.

[[initial-view]]
== Fixed initial view and interaction

Use one orthographic three-quarter view. Let `Lx`, `Ly`, and `Lz` be the exact
city-bounds lengths, and let target `T` be the exact bounds center. The
initial-view descriptor is:

[source,text]
----
target-to-viewer direction D = (1, 1, 1) / sqrt(3)
screen-right R              = (1, 0, -1) / sqrt(2)
screen-up V                 = (-1, 2, -1) / sqrt(6)
E_r                          = (Lx + Lz) / (2 * sqrt(2))
E_v                          = (Lx + 2*Ly + Lz) / (2 * sqrt(6))
----

For every positive viewport aspect `a = width/height`, the orthographic
vertical and horizontal half-sizes are:

[source,text]
----
H = (11/10) * max(E_v, E_r/a)
horizontal half-size = aH
----

Physical camera distance and the clip-plane mechanism are noncanonical
renderer facts fixed for the selected realization by ADR 0008; every city
bound must remain unclipped. Initial publication has no camera
animation. Orbit, pan, zoom, auto-rotation, focus, hover, click, selection, and
camera controls are all absent.

A passive viewport resize only reapplies this fit formula for the latest
positive integer host size and verified positive aspect. It does not rebuild,
reupload, reorder, animate, retry, debounce, or persist the city. Unchanged
size causes no draw. A non-positive/non-integer changed size, drawing-buffer
mismatch, context loss, WebGL error, callback throw, or required resize-draw
failure uses `M1-PRES-1` and the published-presentation transition below.

[[precision-repeatability]]
== Precision and repeatability

Building dimensions, positions, gaps, and bounds are exact integer city units;
derived centers are exact half-integers. They permit no construction tolerance
or rounding. Identity, sequence, dimensions, positions, bounds, palette bytes,
and view-policy fields require exact equality.

Only scalar numerical evaluation of the normalized camera vectors and
projection formulas permits this component-wise tolerance:

[source,text]
----
|actual - expected| <= 10^-9 * max(1, |expected|)
----

Pixel hashes and screenshot equality are not acceptance evidence. For the same
canonical input and positive viewport aspect, repeatability means exact
canonical-scene equality and the same camera evaluation within only that
tolerance. The uploaded `float32` matrix and pixels are renderer facts. Time,
locale, randomness, concurrency, carrier order, and device order cannot enter
the canonical result.

[[city-failure]]
== Atomic construction and presentation failure

[cols="3,2,6",options="header"]
|===
|Category |Code |Governing condition

|`City construction failed`
|`M1-CITY-1`
|Invalid, duplicate, incomplete, empty-at-this-stage, malformed, unsafe,
unrepresentable, missing, additional, repeated, inconsistent, or otherwise
nonconforming canonical input/model; inability to produce the complete exact
model; defensive handoff validation failure or throw; or arithmetic/model
corruption.

|`Presentation failed`
|`M1-PRES-1`
|Only after an already-valid complete model passes defensive validation:
failure of the fixed GPU representation or allocation, canvas or context,
returned attributes, shader/program, upload, draw, framing, passive resize,
context retention, or another required presentation operation.
|===

Each failed stage produces only its one category and code. Neither result
exposes a module path, partial model, partial scene, fallback
layout/palette/camera, prior city, or late result. No building is skipped or
clamped, and no alternate presentation is substituted.

A post-selection failure retains only the current canonical repository URL and
full lowercase commit ID. Replacement and cancellation preserve
xref:04-functional-requirements.adoc#states[M1-STATE-1 through M1-STATE-5],
including latest-attempt precedence and atomic publication, unchanged.

If a valid accepted input exceeds a private implementation capacity, that is a
conformance defect. It does not permit truncation or an undeclared limit or
failure result.

[[post-success-presentation-failure]]
== Published presentation lifecycle

Initial publication is atomic: defensive validation, fixed detached WebGL
staging and draw, context-loss listener registration, successful construction
and observation of one `ResizeObserver`, a final detached host-size reread and
any required redraw, and a final current-generation eligibility check all
precede one synchronous no-yield commit of canvas, identity, and `Success`.
Observer setup after publication is prohibited. A failed or stale candidate
never reaches the DOM.

The current published presentation has exactly these additional transitions:

[source,text]
----
Success
  -- current published WebGL context loss -->
Failure / Presentation failed / M1-PRES-1

Success
  -- required passive-resize presentation failure -->
Failure / Presentation failed / M1-PRES-1
----

Either event synchronously removes the complete city and canvas, disposes the
presentation, and retains the current canonical repository URL, full lowercase
commit ID, and current input draft. It does not restore an earlier city, start
a replacement, retry, select a fallback renderer, or alter a post-submit edit.
The failure remains until resubmit or reload; editing alone does not clear it.
A stale queued loss or resize callback performs only local idempotent disposal
and cannot change visible state.

Normal failure, replacement, cancellation, and reload disconnect the observer,
remove the loss listener and canvas, delete each live shader, program, buffer,
and VAO exactly once, and clear adapter-owned typed data and references. If the
context is already lost, cleanup still removes application-owned DOM,
listeners, observers, and references but makes no driver-release claim.
Cleanup makes no secure-erasure, immediate computation/network-stop, driver-
release-latency, garbage-collection, physical-browser, or operating-system-
process teardown promise.

[[city-decision-record]]
== Decision authority and rationale

After #442, the human decision-maker established: “These are not difficult
decisions and do not need human intervention. If there is a clear
recommendation then it is the Requirements Engineer's job to make decisions.”
That governance decision delegates exact requirements choices when one
recommendation is clear, evidence-backed, deterministic, reversible through
normal requirements revision, inside the accepted outcome, scope, and
non-goals, and neither an excluded high-impact decision nor an unresolved
architecture or technology trade-off.

The accepted https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/444[#444] records
the choices below as exercises of that human delegation, supported by #441
through #443 and their recorded evidence and rationale. They satisfy #442's
required decision for CC-01 through CC-08; the M1 implementation gate in
https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/464[#464] must verify both the
delegation and each exercise before implementation handoff.

[cols="2,4,3,4",options="header"]
|===
|Choice |Rationale |Downside |Rejected alternatives

|Integer prism geometry
|Smallest exact mapping; preserves every metric difference without
renderer-specific rounding.
|Footprint area grows quadratically with executable-unit count, so large modules
can dominate.
|Area-linear square roots and logarithmic compression add rounding or aesthetic
policy.

|Fixed complexity bands
|Equal complexity has the same color across repositories, and power-of-two
bands cover an unbounded metric.
|Values at or above `16` are not distinguished by color.
|Repository-relative gradients make a module depend on peers; red/green risk
semantics are unsupported.

|Buildings only, with inert identities
|Avoids untrusted-label, clutter, hierarchy, and extra model-limit decisions
while retaining exact traceability.
|M1 cannot identify an individual module to the user.
|Static or hover labels add text safety, overlap/layout, and exploration
behavior.

|Canonical square-shelf layout and no independent CC-01 through CC-08 caps
|Preserves canonical order, prevents overlap, is more compact than one row, and
needs no packing heuristic; upstream limits already bound input.
|Rows can be ragged and non-optimal; downstream full-envelope evidence must
renew ADR 0008's accepted visualization-feasibility proof.
|Size sorting, bin packing, hierarchy, and arbitrary lower city caps add
unstable heuristics or unsupported thresholds.

|Fixed orthographic view with no interaction
|Shows height and both footprint axes, preserves comparisons, and avoids
perspective, field-of-view, and input choices.
|There is no exploration, and unusually tall cities can leave blank space.
|Perspective with orbit or zoom expands M1 into exploration.

|Exact scene facts with camera-only numerical tolerance
|Integer facts avoid cross-runtime drift while a narrow tolerance permits
square-root evaluation.
|Evidence is semantic rather than device-pixel identity.
|Rounded geometry loses distinctions; screenshot hashes bind acceptance to
renderer and device behavior.

|One failure per construction or presentation stage
|Preserves #441 atomicity and gives #457 through #460 objective evidence
ownership.
|The user receives no module-specific diagnostic.
|Skipping or clamping buildings, alternate presentation, or retaining an old
city would publish misleading, partial, or stale output.
|===

The accepted https://github.com/FelixGeisler/code-city/issues/451[#451] records
the Requirements Engineer's delegated selection of browser-native WebGL2,
source-free fail-closed presentation, post-`Success` lifecycle refinement, and
explicitly-unnecessary CC-09/PR-01 disposition on the clear Software Architect
recommendation and incorporated Security Reviewer constraints. ADR 0008 is the
authoritative technology decision and needs no later generic human-acceptance
gate.
